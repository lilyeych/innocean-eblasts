Hyundai Canada is proud to announce that Matthew Schaeffer is our new brand ambassador and the face of Hyundai Hope on Wheels. Drafted first overall by the New York Islanders in 2025, the 19-year-old defenceman is poised to lead the next generation of NHL superstars.
Since losing his mother Jennifer to breast cancer in 2024, the Hamilton, ON native has become an ambassador for Hockey Fights Cancer and co-founded the Jennifer Schaefer Child Support Center with Northwell Health in New York, which provides dedicated child care for parents undergoing cancer treatment.
Entering its second year in Canada, Hyundai Hope on Wheels continues its nationwide mission to end childhood cancer. This year, it has committed more than $4.1 million to support children battling cancer at pediatric oncology centres across Canada. Hyundai, its 226 dealers and drivers are making a difference together. When a new Hyundai is sold, a portion of that sale helps to fund critical research and care. Every Hyundai driver brings all of us closer to a future free of pediatric cancer.

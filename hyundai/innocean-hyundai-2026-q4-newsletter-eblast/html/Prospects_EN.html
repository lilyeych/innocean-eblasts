Hyundai Canada is proud to welcome Matthew Schaefer as a new brand ambassador and the face of Hyundai Hope on Wheels. Drafted first overall by the New York Islanders in 2025, the 19-year-old prodigal defenceman is poised to lead the next generation of NHL superstars.
Since losing his mother Jennifer to breast cancer in 2024, the Hamilton, Ont. native has become an advocate for Hockey Fights Cancer and co-founded the Jennifer Schaefer Child Support Center with Northwell Health in New York, which provides dedicated child care for parents undergoing cancer treatment.
Driving Hope Forward
Now, in its second year in Canada, Hyundai Hope on Wheels is continuing its nationwide mission to end childhood cancer.
This year, the foundation has expanded its committed to more than $4.1 million to support funding for life-saving research, paediatric oncology clinical trials, family support programs and transportation assistance initiatives that help children access the care they need.
Supported by Hyundai Auto Canada and its 225 dealers across the country, every new Hyundai sold helps advance this important work.

NAVIGATOR’S NOTES
Move audiences through relevancy.
The Kia team has a tall order for 2026: launch five nameplates, each with a distinct story and audience.
That’s no small task when brands are fighting for attention across more screens, platforms and touchpoints. It’s easy to add to the noise. It’s much harder to make people care.
For us, that’s where cultural relevancy comes in. It means understanding what’s happening in our audience’s world, uncovering the human insights, and finding where they can credibly intersect with our brand.
For our Seltos campaign, we tapped into the feeling of “finding your era”. Rather than simply talking at our target, we connected with the values, language, tensions, and social context shaping how they see themselves and the world around them.
EV4? We partnered with REZZ, stepping into her hypnotic visual world and connecting it with the equally “next level” EV4 to not just talk about it, but be about it.
With EV5, we tapped into a cultural shift: there’s no single definition of family life anymore, and it rarely stands still. Needs shift and roles evolve. Our campaign positioned versatility as more than a product benefit – it became a true reflection of modern family life.
Different nameplates, different audiences, but one shared principle: relevance turns attention into connection.
As we tackle what’s to come, we’ll keep asking: What’s changing in our audience’s world? Why does it matter to them? And where does our brand have a real reason to show up and create momentum?
When we understand what moves people, we can create work that moves everything forward.
Daniel Vescio
Group Account Director
Sea La Vie
Thank you for the music, the songs we sang, and all the joy they brought… 🎵
A few weeks ago, we said Sea La Vie and traded the office for a little Mediterranean escape at our annual INNOCEAN Canada Summer Party. With Mamma Mia energy and Greek island vibes in full swing, The Parlour became our home for an afternoon of good food, featured cocktails, karaoke, games, and even better company.
INNOCEAN Bingo got everyone mixing and mingling beyond their usual teams, with David Nolet and Sal Sharma taking home the wins. And because no Mamma Mia‑inspired party would be complete without a little singing, the karaoke mic stayed busy, with Josh Murray earning the top spot. 🎤
Between photo booth poses, ping pong, cocktails, singalongs, and a little friendly competition, it was great to see everyone step away from their screens and spend some quality time together. More importantly, it gave us a chance to do soju bombs with people we might not get to work with every day.
A huge thank you to everyone who came out and made the afternoon what it was. And an extra special shoutout to our Social Committee for dreaming up the details and bringing Sea La Vie to life.

Until next time, Sea La Vie!

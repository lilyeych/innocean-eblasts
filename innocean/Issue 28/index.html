The Ugly Duckling of Automotive Advertising.
Nobody ever stopped scrolling because they saw a super sexy cabin air filter.
Let's be real. When you think of automotive advertising, you picture sleek vehicles, winding roads, dramatic lighting, and that perfect beauty shot. You probably don't picture winter tires, brake pads, or a complimentary vehicle inspection report.
Welcome to Aftersales. The ugly duckling of automotive advertising.
While everyone else is chasing the next big launch, we're over here figuring out how to make a battery inspection the most interesting thing in someone's feed.
And if you've spent any time around our team, you've probably heard the phrase "Aftersales is fun!" more times than you'd care to admit. Is it genuine enthusiasm or Sarah's ongoing brainwashing campaign? We'll let you decide.
But here's the thing. We actually mean it.
We're not trying to make people want something new. We're trying to make them care about something they already own.
Which, as anyone in this agency knows, is easier said than done.
Our briefs aren't always the sexiest, but they definitely keep us on our toes. One minute we're talking winter tires, the next we're debating how to make vehicle protection feel exciting. Throw in a few rounds of feedback, a tight turnaround, and the occasional "can we make it pop?" and you've got yourself a typical Tuesday.
But that's the fun of it. Aftersales forces us to find the interesting angle when there isn't an obvious one. To take something practical, give it a little personality, and hopefully make someone care before their check-engine light does the job for us.
So, we'll leave you with this thought.... We spend so much time thinking about which vehicle to buy. But once the keys are in our hands, how much thought do we give to what keeps us on the road?
Sarah Goldie
Account Director
